# GHI Calculator

Draft tool for the Gill Health Initiative (GHI) review of gill health in farmed salmonids, 2007-2026.

**Live page:** https://fomedi.github.io/ghi-calculator/

It has two linked parts:

1. **Case definition**: classifies a gill sample as not a case, suspect, probable, confirmed (named agent) or multifactorial (CGD) from clinical signs, gross gill score, graded histopathology, qPCR and ISH/IHC.
2. **Risk calculator**: an evidence-weighted points score for a cage's short-term risk of gill disease, using environmental, agent, host and husbandry factors identified in a screening of 319 articles (WoS, Scopus, PubMed).

## Status

Draft v0.1 for expert review (Delphi). All thresholds and points are placeholders, editable in the page, and **not validated**. The risk score is a relative ranking, not a probability. Not for diagnostic or regulatory use.

## Use

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the live page. Edits to thresholds and points are stored only in the viewer's browser.

## Citation

Mardones F. et al. (in preparation). Gill health research in farmed salmon, 2007-2026: a bibliometric review toward a science-based case definition.
