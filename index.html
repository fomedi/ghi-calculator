    Summary

    Draft tiered case definition

    Built from the three lines of evidence measured in the 2007-2026 literature review: gross or clinical signs, a compatible lesion, and the agent within the lesion. Only 6% of 231 disease-focused articles reported all three. Thresholds in brackets are the current defaults and can be changed in the case tab.

    Recorded at every tier

    Production stage (freshwater, RAS or sea), water temperature and salinity, plankton and jellyfish events, treatments and net cleaning in the previous two weeks, and the scales and assays used. These records are what make cases comparable across farms and countries, and they are the inputs of the risk calculator.

    Evidence behind each factor

    From the screening of 319 articles (2007-2026; WoS, Scopus, PubMed) and extraction of 189 risk-factor findings, of which 82 were field associations. Effects come from abstracts and use different outcome definitions, so they indicate direction, not comparable magnitudes.
